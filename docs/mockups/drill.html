6. Past-paper answer-writing drill

CS and CMA theory papers are won by writing. YouTube gives no practice.

  • A real past question (linked to ICSI's official paper), with marks and a timer.
  • She writes on paper, snaps a photo of her own sheet.
  • Her own notes on that chapter appear beside it, with a 4-point checklist.
  • A miss goes into her mistake book and comes back in 3, 10 and 30 days.

We link to official question papers rather than copying them, until ICSI's terms are checked.
