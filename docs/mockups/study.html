2. The study page

YouTube's own player on top, untouched. Under it, one thumb bar. She taps while she listens and fills notes in later.

  • Mark: one tap saves the second. Long-press for a type (Def, Sec, PYQ, Trick).
  • Doubt: saves the second and one line; "Parked, keep going".
  • ★: important for the hall sheet. −10s: jump back.
  • At a pause, the tray replays 10 seconds around each mark so she can write one line.
  • Long-press a word in a note → it becomes a card.

Nothing ever sits on top of the player (rule R7). Notes store only the video ID and the second (R1).
