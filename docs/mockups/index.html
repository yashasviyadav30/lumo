FocusLearn: study companion mockups

Static screens to show students, not the real app. The dark box stands for YouTube's own player, which we never change. All study content (CSR rules, amendment names, questions, names like "Riya") is sample text for the mockup and has not been checked.

The idea in one line

YouTube is where the lectures live. FocusLearn is where you remember them. Mark moments while you watch, revise from your own cards, replay only the part you forgot, get doubts answered, and track your attempt.

1. Home

One thing to do now: resume, 5 cards, a doubt answered.

 2. Study page

Player on top; Mark · Doubt · ★ · −10s under it.

 3. My cards

Swipe your own notes; "Watch this bit" replays 90 seconds.

 4. Doubts

Pinned to the second; a friend answers from WhatsApp.

 5. Personal

Notebook, R1/R2/R3 tracker, amendments for your attempt.

 6. Past-paper drill

Write an answer on paper, check it against your notes.
