## 2. Merged ideas

Switch score: 5 = she'd open it daily and nothing else does it; 3 = useful, she'd use it if she's already here; 1 = nice to have. Effort for one developer: Low = days, Medium = 1–3 weeks, High = a month or more, or work that isn't code (sales, moderation).

### M-01. One-tap capture under the player
- **What:** A thumb bar below the player: Mark · Doubt · ★ · −10 s. Long-press Mark for types (Def, Sec, PYQ, Trick, "Samajh nahi aaya"). Marks are empty on purpose; when she pauses or the lecture ends, a tray asks "6 marks: fill them in?" and replays 10 s around each. Laptop gets keys (N, D, H). On return after leaving the tab: "Rewind to 32:10?".
- **Sources:** A1, A5, A7, A9, NIGHT-09, NIGHT-18, TOPPER-05, SCI-13, SCI-17, SCI-18, DESIGN-03, DESIGN-04.
- **Why she'd switch:** She asked for notes next to the lecture, and this is faster than pausing to write. But it's table stakes: Pi Lens and SyncStudy already have notes beside the video.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ✅ R7 (below the player, our buttons call the API; no auto-pause). R1 (video ID + seconds + her text).
- **Beats:** SyncStudy and Pi Lens on speed (one tap, no typing mid-lecture) and on types that feed revision. Not on anything else.

### M-02. Swipe my own cards
- **What:** A full-screen vertical feed of *her* material: cloze cards (long-press a word in a note to blank it), open doubts, sure/guess before reveal. "Watch this bit" jumps to the second. Cards retire after 3 correct recalls on 3 days. Mixed across papers. Filter "Sec only" for law drills.
- **Sources:** D1, D2, NIGHT-07, TOPPER-07, TOPPER-23, SCI-07, SCI-08, SCI-10, DESIGN-05.
- **Why she'd switch:** Her thumb wants to swipe at 11 pm; this gives the swipe to her own ESG notes, 5 minutes, and it ends. It's the one reason to open the app on days she watches nothing.
- **Switch score:** 4. **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ✅ Only her text (R3, R4). No streaks or points (R8). Own visual design, no thumbnails.
- **Beats:** Anki on setup (one long-press, no deck building) and jump-back to the lecture. Pi Lens's flashcards come from the video; ours are what *she* found important. Honest limit: it is only as good as the notes she wrote.

### M-03. Test before you rewatch, replay only the miss
- **What:** Opening a finished lecture offers "60 seconds first: what do you remember?", then shows her notes beside it with "replay 12:10–13:40" chips for gaps. A failed card replays a window (30 s before the note to 60 s after), then asks the card again.
- **Sources:** A10, D3, D4, SCI-04, SCI-05.
- **Why she'd switch:** Re-watching a 2-hour lecture before exams becomes 1 minute of recall plus 10 minutes of the bits she forgot. YouTube can only replay the whole thing.
- **Switch score:** 4 near exams, 2 other weeks; counted as 3. **Effort:** Low, once M-01 and M-02 exist.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ An offer below the player with one-tap dismiss, never a gate (R8). Prefer the embed's `start`/`end` parameters, or a window she starts, over our code pausing mid-play.
- **Beats:** Nobody does this. It needs her timestamped notes, which is our data.

### M-04. One card a day, at her hour
- **What:** One web push a day at a time she picks. The push holds one of her cards; she answers from the lock screen. Optional bedtime 5 and the same 5 next morning. A plan item can deep-link to "ESG lecture 4 at 12:10". Backs off after 3 ignored pushes.
- **Sources:** NIGHT-19, NIGHT-20, SCI-16, SCI-20, DESIGN-12.
- **Why she'd switch:** The buzz on her phone becomes study instead of a YouTube notification.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Medium (push scheduling; iPhone only after home-screen install 🟡; action buttons vary ❓).
- **Rules:** ✅ Card from her notes (R4). Push text never holds a video title (R1, R11). No streak copy.
- **Beats:** Anki and Duolingo pushes on content (hers, tied to a lecture), not on mechanics.

### M-05. Doubts pinned to the second, answered by a friend
- **What:** "Doubt" saves the second and one line, then "Parked, keep going". A Doubts board shows open and solved. Any doubt becomes a WhatsApp link: the friend opens a page with the official player at that second and a box to answer. The answer lands on her doubt.
- **Sources:** A4, B4, NIGHT-15, NIGHT-16, DESIGN-07, CONTRA-21.
- **Why she'd switch:** Her doubt goes to the three people who will answer, at the exact moment, not into a comment pile. Every share also brings a classmate to the app.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Low (marker and board) plus Medium (public answer page with rate limits).
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Answerers who aren't signed in must tick 18+ before we keep their text (R10). Report button on answers. The page embeds the player and never covers it (R7).
- **Beats:** YouTube comments (buried) and WhatsApp alone (no timestamp, no board). Nobody links a doubt to a second.

### M-06. Mistake book with re-attempt dates
- **What:** After a mock or PYQ, log each miss in 10 seconds: her words or a photo of her own sheet, chapter, cause (concept / forgot / silly / time), and optionally the lecture second. It comes back at 3, 10 and 30 days. A simple mock-score line per paper.
- **Sources:** TOPPER-03, TOPPER-15, SCI-24, CONTRA-12.
- **Why she'd switch:** Toppers keep one; average students skip it because it's a chore. Notion users already build it by hand ✅.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Low–Medium (reuses M-02's scheduler; photo storage on R2 later).
- **Rules:** ✅ Her data only. Photos of her paper, never the video.
- **Beats:** Notion on no setup, spaced return and a jump to the teacher's explanation. Not a daily pull until mocks start.

### M-07. PYQ answer-writing drill
- **What:** She picks a past question from ICSI/ICMAI's public paper (we link the PDF; she or a senior types the question and tags year and marks). A timer; she writes on paper, snaps it, then sees her own notes on that chapter beside the photo and ticks a 4-point checklist. Chapters show "asked 4 times".
- **Sources:** D5, NIGHT-24, TOPPER-04, TOPPER-16, SCI-26, CONTRA-04.
- **Why she'd switch:** CS and CMA theory papers are won by writing; YouTube gives zero practice. "Knew it but couldn't write it" (NIGHT-05) is the exact pain.
- **Switch score:** 4. **Effort:** Medium for the code; the real cost is someone typing questions per paper.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Link to official papers; don't copy question text in bulk until ICSI/ICMAI terms are checked ❓.
- **Beats:** Pi Lens has PYQs, but for school/JEE/NEET and not for answer writing. Coaching test series cost money.

### M-08. Revision tracker on the syllabus
- **What:** Each paper's official topics as a list. Per topic: R1/R2/R3 ticks with dates, a red/amber/green tap after each revision, "last checked 12 days ago". One number per paper: "cards you can recall today". A pace line: "R2 needs 4 chapters a week; you did 2."

- **Why she'd switch:** The page every aspirant draws by hand, ready-made from the syllabus maps we already have.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ✅ User-marked; nothing guessed from watching; no rewards (R8).
- **Beats:** Excel/Notion on setup time and on the link to her notes and cards. SyncStudy has JEE/NEET trackers, not CS/CMA 🟡.

### M-09. Exam countdown mode
- **What:** Exam date and attempt at setup (repeaters add last marks per paper and one "what went wrong" chip). Home changes by stage: T-30 finish R2, T-15 reds only, T-3 mistakes and Sec cards, T-1 a one-page hall sheet. Card gaps shrink as the date nears. Optional printable weekly plan.
- **Sources:** B7, C3, C5, NIGHT-05, TOPPER-17, SCI-23, DESIGN-25, CONTRA-08, CONTRA-16.
- **Why she'd switch:** In the last month it tells her what to do each day, from her own reds and mistakes.
- **Switch score:** 3. **Effort:** Medium, and only after M-02, M-06, M-08.
- **Rules:** ✅ Suggestions only; nothing locks (R8).
- **Beats:** No rival does exam-stage modes for CS/CMA 🔵.

### M-10. Home opens on one thing to do
- **What:** Home is one big card: "Resume Sec 135 at 42:10", or "4 cards · 2 min", or "1 doubt open", chosen by time of day and her data. "Too tired? 5 cards instead" below it. After a gap: "Welcome back, want a 3-day restart?" with no red marks. "My day ends at 4 am" setting. "Taking tonight off" counts as rest. First run: an 18+ tick, then straight into a lecture with the note bar; sign-up comes after the first note.
- **Sources:** A8, C4, NIGHT-01, NIGHT-02, NIGHT-08, TOPPER-08, SCI-19, SCI-22, DESIGN-01, DESIGN-02, DESIGN-11, DESIGN-13, CONTRA-18, CONTRA-25.
- **Why she'd switch:** The first thing she sees is where she stopped, not a search bar. That is the direct fix for "it doesn't feel different from YouTube".
- **Switch score:** 3 (it presents other features; alone it's empty). **Effort:** Low.
- **Rules:** ✅ R1 (IDs + seconds; titles re-fetched). R8: a pointer, no reward; "Play" is always one tap. R10: 18+ before any data.
- **Beats:** YouTube's Home on intent. SyncStudy has resume too; ours adds cards and doubts.

### M-11. Backlog triage by swipe
- **What:** Import the playlist she's behind on. Swipe each lecture: must watch / skim at 2x / skip (one-shot covers it). Then the maths: "23 lectures ≈ 31 h → 11 days at your pace; you have 9. Move 3 to one-shot?"
- **Sources:** NIGHT-03, NIGHT-04, TOPPER-14, SCI-21, DESIGN-14, CONTRA-09.
- **Why she'd switch:** "Backlog" is everyday speech for aspirants; this turns 100 lectures into a finite plan in five minutes.
- **Switch score:** 3 (episodic, not daily). **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ She judges each lecture, never our AI (R3). Durations are YouTube data: use them, don't keep them past 30 days; store only her piles and our totals (R1).
- **Beats:** YouTube Watch Later (a guilt list that only grows). SyncStudy tracks progress but doesn't triage 🟡.

### M-12. Accountability pod with a nightly check-in
- **What:** A pod of 3–5 on the same paper and attempt, started from a link pasted into her existing WhatsApp group (strangers later). Each night: "What did you study?" in one line, logged against the syllabus; the pod sees done / partly / rest, nothing else. First channel: a free Telegram bot, before building it into the app.
- **Sources:** E1, NIGHT-12, TOPPER-25, SCI-25, DESIGN-08, DESIGN-24, CONTRA-01, CONTRA-02.
- **Why she'd switch:** Someone notices if she skips tonight. YPT's top 5★ reason is groups ✅.
- **Switch score:** 4 if the pod is people she knows; 1 if it's empty. **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Opt-in, 18+ (R10), study actions only, no ranking (R8), no video IDs in pod events or bot logs (R11).
- **Beats:** YPT on being tied to her syllabus and paper, on laptop too, with a "today" she sets. Loses to YPT on existing users.

### M-13. Silent study room
- **What:** A presence-only room per exam: "23 CS aspirants studying · 7 on ESG". No chat, no video, a few KB a minute.
- **Sources:** NIGHT-11, TEACH-20, CONTRA-14.
- **Why she'd switch:** Company at 1 am, free.
- **Switch score:** 2 (an empty room at 1 am is worse than none). **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Show the paper, never the video (R1, R11). No per-lecture viewer counts ❓. 18+.
- **Beats:** StudyStream on data use and price. Loses to YPT and StudyStream on crowd size, which is the whole product.

### M-14. Weekly share card
- **What:** Once a week, a plain card she can forward: "ESG ch 3–4 revised, 41 cards, 2 doubts solved." To a parent, sibling or pod. She edits before sending. A monthly "Wrapped" later.
- **Sources:** NIGHT-13, DESIGN-06, CONTRA-13.

- **Switch score:** 2. **Effort:** Low (Web Share API, text or image).
- **Rules:** ✅ Study actions only, no watch time, no titles (R1, R8).
- **Beats:** Nothing similar; low stakes either way.

### M-15. Amendment tracker per attempt (CS, CMA, CA)
- **What:** For her attempt, a checklist of amendments that apply, each linked to the official notice, marked seen / studied / revised. Beside a lecture: "Uploaded Mar 2025; your attempt covers amendments up to [date]. Check the list." Later, teachers pin "22:00–31:40 changed; see the update".
- **Sources:** NIGHT-23, TOPPER-24, TEACH-05, CONTRA-07.
- **Why she'd switch:** YouTube never tells her a lecture predates the law she'll be tested on. For a law paper that can decide a pass.
- **Switch score:** 3 (high trust, not daily). **Effort:** Low code; Medium ongoing (someone curates each attempt, probably the sister).
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Comparing `publishedAt` with a date is arithmetic, but the wording must be "uploaded before", never "outdated" (R3). Don't keep `publishedAt` past 30 days (R1). A wrong list costs marks: show who made it and when.
- **Beats:** Nobody 🔵. ICSI publishes notices, but no per-attempt checklist tied to lectures.

### M-16. Teacher class kit with a link
- **What:** A teacher (or assistant) pastes a playlist, orders it, adds chapter times, a notes PDF per lecture, and 3–5 questions per lecture. They share one link or class code in the pinned comment. Students who join get the path, notes and questions ready, plus "sir's pace".
- **Sources:** E3, TEACH-01, 02, 03, 04, 12, 14, 15, 21, 25, TOPPER-18, DESIGN-18, CONTRA-06.
- **Why she'd switch:** Her own teacher tells her to. That is the strongest reason there is, and the only one that brings students we didn't recruit.
- **Switch score:** 5 if a teacher she follows does it; 0 otherwise. **Effort:** High (the dashboard is Medium; getting teachers is sales).
- **Rules:** ✅ Teacher's own data under a written agreement; order and labels are a human's (R3). Only IDs kept long term (R1).
- **Beats:** SyncStudy's uncurated public courses; Pi Lens (PW's own world). A mid-size CS/CMA teacher has no tool like this 🔵.

### M-17. Teacher feedback loop
- **What:** Aggregated, consented doubt clusters per lecture (a bucket shows only at 10+ students), weekly top doubts, topic-done rates, a Monday email. Later: senior TAs and model notes.
- **Sources:** TEACH-08, 09, 10, 13, 19, 22.
- **Why she'd switch:** Indirect: her doubt reaches the teacher.
- **Switch score:** 2 (needs M-16 at scale). **Effort:** Medium.
- **Rules:** ⚠️ Consent per doubt; minimum group size; DPDP consent 🟡; lives in the database, not logs (R11).

### M-18. Share-to-study from the YouTube app

## 4. Rule traps found

Ideas the personas marked ✅, or marked ⚠️ for a different reason, that break or risk a rule.

| Idea(s) | Persona said | The trap | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCI-02, SCI-13, SCI-04 | ✅ / ⚠️ R7 only | **Our code pauses the player on a timer**, not on her tap. R7 allows our *buttons* to call the API. | CONTRA-03, TOPPER-09 | ✅ / ⚠️ R1 | **"Which syllabus topic it belongs to"**: if the app suggests a topic from the shared title, that's judging a video from its title (R3). | She picks the topic. We never pre-fill from the title. |
| CONTRA-21 | ✅ | **Anonymous answer page** keeps text from people who never passed an 18+ check (R10). DESIGN-07 caught this; CONTRA-21 didn't. | 18+ tick before any answer is stored; rate limits; report button. |
| A7, SCI-15, CONTRA-11 | ✅ | **Chrome's speech-to-text sends audio to Google's servers** 🟡. Not our zero-retention LLM path. | Say so in the privacy notice, or make voice optional and off by default. |

## 5. The uncomfortable truths

1. **Nothing here makes her leave YouTube, and nothing can.** The lectures stay on YouTube, the ads play, and the player's links open the YouTube app. The honest goal is to become where she goes *before and after* a lecture: revision, doubts, check-ins. Measure daily opens for revision, not "switched from YouTube".

2. **"Notes beside the video" is table stakes, not a moat.** Pi Lens (PW-backed, 100,000+ installs, free) and SyncStudy already do it, and Pi Lens fills its panel automatically from the video, which we are banned from doing. Our only defensible edges are slow ones: months of her own notes (switching cost), CS/CMA specifics (amendments, syllabus), and people (friends, teachers).

3. **Almost every strong idea depends on her writing notes, and the research says note-taking is the pain.** No notes means no cards, no replay-the-miss, no doubts board. If CS students won't mark and fill notes in week 2, the whole revision stack is empty. Test that with 5 real students before building M-02.

4. **Social features die without density.** A pod for "CS Prof, ESG, Dec 2026" of strangers needs hundreds of users on that exact paper; a silent room with 2 people at 1 am feels worse than no room. Start with friends she already has (a WhatsApp link), or don't build it.

5. **The one big growth lever is sales, not code, and nobody has done it.** A teacher saying "open this in FocusLearn" beats every feature on this list. Not one teacher has been asked. One conversation with a mid-size CS/CMA teacher is worth more than the next month of coding.

6. **The evidence is thin, and the personas graded their own homework.** One real user, no Reddit, almost no first-person CS/CMA quotes, and most personas marked their own ideas ✅. My switch scores are judgement 🔵, not data. The hand-run pilot (M-26) and the sister's five questions should come before more building.
