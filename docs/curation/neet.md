## Syllabus source

- **Body:** National Medical Commission (Under Graduate Medical Education Board). NTA runs the exam and publishes the NMC letter.
- **Document:** "Updated syllabus for NEET(UG)-2026", NMC letter U-14023/19/NEET (UG Exam)/UGMEB, dated 22-12-2025, published by NTA on 08-01-2026: <https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260108180635.pdf>
- **Shape:** Physics 20 units, Chemistry 20 units, Biology 10 units. Botany and Zoology are not split in the official text, so they are search terms on the Biology topic.
- ❓ I found no 2027 syllabus. Coaching pages say NEET 2027 follows the 2026 one. nmc.org.in did not load cleanly, so I used NTA's copy of the NMC letter.

## Topic map (53 topics)

Levels: `phy` Physics, `chem` Chemistry, `bio` Biology. Each subject has one parent topic (`neet-phy-physics`, `neet-chem-chemistry`, `neet-bio-biology`) and one child per official unit. The names are short and in our own words.

- **Physics (20):** measurement, kinematics, laws of motion, work-energy-power, rotation, gravitation, solids and liquids, thermodynamics, kinetic theory, oscillations and waves, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, EMI and AC, EM waves, optics, dual nature, atoms and nuclei, semiconductor electronics, experimental skills.
- **Chemistry (20):** basic concepts, atomic structure, bonding, thermodynamics, solutions, equilibrium, redox and electrochemistry, kinetics, periodicity, p-block, d- and f-block, coordination compounds, purification of organic compounds, GOC, hydrocarbons, halogen compounds, oxygen compounds, nitrogen compounds, biomolecules, practical chemistry.
- **Biology (10):** diversity of life, structure of plants and animals, cell, plant physiology, human physiology, reproduction, genetics and evolution, biology and human welfare, biotechnology, ecology.
